## English
November 1975. Aziz, sixteen, rides a bus through a foggy forest with his mother. They are going to visit his grandfather at the Oromgoh sanatorium.

The building is empty. The lights flicker. Someone is screaming in room 139.

*The Last Shake* is a short story-driven psychological horror played from a wheelchair. Explore a four-storey Soviet sanatorium, read what the old man left behind, run from things you can't explain, and find out what really happened in those last twenty minutes.

- A short, story-driven game
- Full subtitles in English, Russian and Uzbek
- Headphones recommended

*The game was made entirely by a 16-year-old solo developer with a physical disability.*

Здание пустое. Свет мигает. В 139-й кто-то кричит.

*The Last Shake* — короткий сюжетный психологический хоррор от лица парня на коляске. Четыре этажа советского санатория, записки деда, погони, которым нет объяснения — и последние двадцать минут, о которых вы узнаете в конце.
